Columbia University · Meta AI

I am a graduate student at Columbia, part of the LIINC lab advised by Paul Sajda. I work on reinforcement learning for language models, currently focusing on training exploratory reasoning and scientific discovery. Previously, I spent time at the ADDReB Lab at Stanford Medicine, supervised by Jayakumar Rajadas.
